## BACKGROUND

- Elias had been freelancing independently for just over four years.
- His income was relatively steady.
- During the previous six months, his average monthly invoicing had been approximately **€5,600 before tax**.
- In the month before accepting Harbor & Field, he had invoiced approximately **€6,200**.
- He had about **€19,000 in personal and business savings combined**.
- He had no overdue rent, debt payments or urgent bills when he accepted the job.
- He already had approximately **€4,400 of confirmed work** booked for April and May.
- Harbor & Field was not needed to cover an immediate financial shortfall.
- Elias knew the client through Tomás, another freelancer he had worked alongside on two previous projects.
- He had never worked for Clara or Harbor & Field before.
- He had met Clara once at an industry event approximately a year earlier but had not spoken with her for more than a few minutes.
- Elias did not publish fixed project prices on his website.
- His website described his services but gave no pricing.
- He had a private day rate that he sometimes used when quoting larger agency projects, but he did not send it to Harbor & Field.
- His usual day rate at that time was **€480**.
- Before quoting €750, Elias spent about 20 minutes looking through Harbor & Field's public website.
- He did not log into the WordPress backend until after the price had already been accepted.
- No development work was done before the price was agreed.
- The original written scope was a homepage cleanup, mobile spacing fixes, team-page update, 3 recent projects and repair of the contact form.
- The job was largely functional and visually complete by **27 April**.
- Final launch happened on **29 April**.
- A minor post-launch mobile correction was made on **4 May**.
- Harbor & Field continued using the site after launch.
- More than two years later, the core homepage structure, portfolio template, Press page and Approach page created during the project were still live.
- The client continued adding new projects using the template Elias built.
- Elias did not retain enough records to reconstruct exactly how much time he spent reading and responding to WhatsApp voice notes from Clara because some of them were later deleted from the chat.
