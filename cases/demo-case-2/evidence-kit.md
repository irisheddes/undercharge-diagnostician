# BACKGROUND

**Financial state when she said yes.** In September 2024 Joana had about 600 € in her current account and 1.200 € outstanding from a previous client (an agency in Lisbon) who had gone quiet on two payment reminders and would eventually pay in February 2025. Her rent was 480 €, due on the 5th. She had no other work booked for October. She replied to Rui's price question at 00:14.

**How she knew the client.** Rui is the boyfriend of Marta, who is a friend of Sofia, who Joana studied with. They had met once, at Sofia's birthday in July 2024, and spoke for maybe ten minutes. He got her number from Marta.

**Published prices or rate card.** None. Joana's Instagram bio said "video / porto / dm". Her website was a Squarespace page with a showreel and a contact form, no pricing, no packages. She had no written day rate, no hourly rate, and no minimum. When asked what things cost, she worked it out in her head each time, usually against what the client seemed able to pay. The 850 € figure was arrived at in bed, on her phone, in under twenty minutes, with no reference to any previous project.

**Work done before the price was agreed.** She downloaded and watched roughly two hours of the June footage on 20–21 September, and cut a 25-second test sequence to see whether the material could carry a film at all. She sent a still from it to Rui as a WhatsApp image on 21/09 with the message `there's good stuff in here`. The test edit and the discovery call total about 4 hours. Neither was mentioned again, by either party.

**Essentially finished vs. delivered.** The main film, the cutdowns, the bumper and the subtitles were locked on 12 November. The launch had been on the 8th; Mother Culture posted a phone-shot Reel instead. The final handover folder went out on 27 November, because Joana was waiting on a clean vector version of the new logo, which Rui had promised on the 13th and sent on the 25th. In the two-week gap she did not invoice, did not chase after the first reminder, and started no other work, because she expected the file "any day".

**Whether the client still uses the result.** Yes. As of early 2026 the 74-second film is the header video on motherculture.pt and the pinned post on the brand's Instagram. The 15-second brewing cut was used as a paid ad in a campaign in spring 2025 with a media spend of roughly 2.000 €. The 2-minute founder cut was shown at the funding meeting; Mother Culture received the funding and now has six employees. Two of Joana's still frames appear on the bottle labels' outer carton. She has never been credited by name in any post or on the site. Rui has recommended her twice, both times to people who then asked for "something like what you did for Mother Culture, but smaller budget".

**Never written down, not reconstructable.** What was actually said in the car park after the funding meeting on 14 November. Rui had driven to Porto and they had a coffee; he walked her to her car and talked for about ten minutes about 2025. Joana came away believing she had been offered a retainer for the following year's content, something in the region of monthly work. Rui, asked about it later, remembered saying that they *hoped* to do more once the funding cleared. There is no message, no email, no note, and no third person present. Nothing came of it either way; the 2025 content was made in-house by a marketing hire.

Also missing: the actual start date. Joana has 20 September in her invoice notes and 24 September in her calendar, and cannot say which is right.
