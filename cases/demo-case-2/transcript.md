DIAGNOSIS
Primary cause: Joana took this job because September's money made losing it
unaffordable — 600 € in the account, 480 € rent due on the 5th, 1.200 € stuck with a
silent client, nothing booked for October — so a walk-away price never existed, and
every number and every boundary downstream was set by someone who could not risk the
deal (F1, necessity acceptance).

HOW I KNOW
Walking the chain backward from the invoice:

The invoice. `INV 2024-041 — Launch video — 850,00 € + IVA` against Joana's own
reconstruction of 78.5–94.5 hours ("call it 85. probably more"). Effective rate: 9–11 €
an hour. That number is the symptom. The question is which link upstream broke first.

Scope shifts. Eight additions — vertical cutdowns, a 14-point revision round, rounds
3–5, the packaging re-edit, PT+EN subtitles, the bumper, the founder cut, the stills —
and the record states it flatly: "Never raised, by either side, at any point: an
additional fee, a revised total, a new invoice line, or a written change to the 850 €."
This is silent scope growth (F8), and it is where most of the money went: the scope
priced on 23/09 — the 60-second film plus one round — accounts for roughly 36–40 of the
85 hours. About half the hours were never in the priced scope at all.

But this link cannot be the primary cause, because unlike engagements where nothing
exists for a change-order to invoke, here a boundary existed in writing. The 23/09
email is precise: "launch video from your June footage, around 60 seconds, for site and
social. 850 € + IVA ... One round of changes included." Joana held that boundary in her
own sent-mail folder and never once invoked it — not at "we'll need it vertical for
stories right?", not at the 6-minute-40 voice note with fourteen changes, not at "do
the subtitles come with it?". She did not need a rate card to answer those messages;
she could have produced a price for the additions the same way she produced the 850 —
on the spot. What she could not produce was the willingness to risk the deal. The link
above must explain that.

The agreed price. Rui asked at 23:47; Joana answered at 00:14 — twenty-seven minutes
later, "in bed, on her phone, in under twenty minutes, with no reference to any
previous project." Her message: "I'd say around 850 for the edit + one round of
changes" followed immediately by "that ok?" — the seller asking the buyer to approve
her own price. Rui accepted with no negotiation ("850 works 👍") and, three minutes
after accepting, dangled the future: "also if this goes well we have the whole content
thing for next year." For a freelancer with an empty October, that sentence priced
every later boundary conversation: each one now risked not just this deal but the
imagined next year of work.

The anchor. None existed — the 850 referenced no market number, no previous project,
no client-side value (F4 is present). But the absence is downstream of the necessity:
an anchor takes time and the nerve to delay, and a same-night midnight reply, sent by
someone whose rent was due, had room for neither.

Pre-agreement work. Before any terms existed, Joana downloaded the footage, watched
two hours of it, and cut a 25-second test — about 4 unpaid hours (F3 is present).
Working to secure the job before money is discussed is the necessity signature in
miniature: the deal was already being protected before it existed.

The decision to take the job. The BACKGROUND is unambiguous: "about 600 € in her
current account and 1.200 € outstanding from a previous client ... rent was 480 €, due
on the 5th. She had no other work booked for October." This is the earliest link, and
it is broken in exactly the way F1 describes: negotiation without a walk-away position
(the BATNA-less negotiation — Fisher & Ury, per the evidence base). Every link below
reads as its consequence — the free test edit, the midnight quote calibrated to a
client who had already said "nothing crazy, we're tiny 😅", the "that ok?", the eight
silences, and the collection posture at the end: film locked 12/11, no invoice for two
weeks while waiting on a logo "any day", one reminder, no new work started. That is not
the behaviour of someone with an alternative. It is the behaviour the client-side
record quietly confirms: instant acceptance of the first number, no counter, and a
pricing conversation two messages long against a scope conversation that ran for weeks.

The chosen-discount check. The kit shows no evidence of a chosen, informed discount:
no "for you" pricing language, no portfolio framing, and a relationship — boyfriend of
a friend of a friend, met once for ten minutes — too thin to carry one. This was not
strategy, and it was not F6. The engagement was underpriced, and the underpricing was
decided the day a job Joana could not afford to lose walked in.

CONTRIBUTING (not primary)

- F8 silent scope growth — where roughly half the hours went, but each absorption was a

- F4 no anchor — the 850 referenced nothing, but a same-night reply under rent pressure

  pricing at 00:14, not the reason the engagement existed on these terms.
- F3 free discovery — 4 unpaid hours before any agreement; small in hours, and itself

- F5 effort-priced outcome work — the film is now the client's header video, a paid-ad

WHAT THIS DIAGNOSIS CANNOT TELL YOU
What Rui would have paid. "850 works 👍" within hours, with no counter, suggests the
number sat under his ceiling — but the kit contains no budget signal from September

What was said in the car park on 14 November. It is unrecoverable by both parties'

The exact hours. The reconstruction is ranged, one Saturday lives on a wiped laptop,

Whether taking the job was wrong. That the engagement was underpriced, and why, is
establishable; whether accepting it was still the right call in September 2024 depends
on facts outside these artifacts. The kit offers one caution rather than an answer:
both referrals it produced asked for "something like what you did for Mother Culture,

anything above it.

The true start date. 20 or 24 September — the kit cannot say. Neither changes the
order of any link in the chain.
